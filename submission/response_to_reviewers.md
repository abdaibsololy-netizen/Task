# 🗣️ الرد على المحكمين | Response to Reviewers

> أنشئ جدولاً لكل ملاحظة: **التعليق → الرد → التعديل ومكانه**. الرد الجيد يحوّل major revision إلى قبول.
> Structure every comment as: **Comment → Response → Change & location**.

---

## مقدمة الرد | Opening letter

Dear [Editor],

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript *"[Title]"* (Ref: [MS number]).
We are grateful to the editor and the reviewers for their time and for the insightful comments, which have materially improved the paper.

We have addressed **all [N] comments**. Below we reproduce each comment verbatim, followed by our response and the changes made (with page/line numbers referring to the revised manuscript). All changes are also visible in the tracked-changes version.

Summary of the most substantial changes:
1. **[Change 1]** (pp. x–y)
2. **[Change 2]** (pp. x–y)
3. **[Change 3]** (pp. x–y)

Sincerely,
[Authors]

## عبارات جاهزة | Useful phrasing

| الموقف | العبارة |
|---|---|
| قبول التعديل | *"We agree and have revised the manuscript accordingly (p. X)."* |
| تعديل جزئي | *"We agree in part. While …, we argue that … and have added a caveat on p. X."* |
| رفض بأدب | *"We respectfully disagree, for the following reason … We have clarified this point on p. X to prevent misreading."* |
| طلب إيضاح | *"We thank the reviewer for raising this important point, which we had not stated explicitly. We now define … on p. X."* |
| خارج النطاق | *"This is an excellent suggestion; given the scope of the present study we have added it to the future-research agenda (Section 7.5)."* |
| بيانات/تحليل إضافي | *"Following this comment, we re-ran the analysis with …; the results (Table 4) confirm …"* |

## ✅ قائمة تحقق للرد | Rebuttal checklist

- [ ] رددت على **كل** تعليق — حتى التي ترفضها.
- [ ] كل رد يشير إلى **مكان التعديل** (صفحة/سطر أو رقم قسم).
- [ ] أرفقت نسخة بتتبع التغييرات **ونسخة نظيفة**.
- [ ] نبرة الرد مهنية ولا جدال شخصي.
- [ ] الالتزامات ("سنضيف دراسة جديدة") واقعية خلال مهلة المراجعة.
- [ ] راجعها المشرف قبل الإرسال.
- [ ] التعديلات مطابقة لما تقوله في الجدول (المحكمون يتحققون).
