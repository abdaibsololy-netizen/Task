# Abstract

> {اكتبه **أخيراً** — بعد اكتمال النتائج. ١٥٠–٢٥٠ كلمة، جملة واحدة لكل عنصر:
> Background → Gap → Objective → Method → Key result → Implication.
> Write it LAST, once results are known. One sentence per element.}

**Background.**
{جملة واحدة: أهمية الذكاء الاصطناعي التوليدي وانتشاره في السياق الذي تدرسه.
Example starter: "Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) has moved from experimental pilots to …"}

**Gap.**
{جملة واحدة بالأرقام من `tools/litkit.py gaps`:
"However, the literature remains fragmented: of the N studies reviewed, only X% …"}

**Objective.**
{جملة واحدة بسؤال البحث: "This study therefore asks: RQ1 …; RQ2 …"}

**Method.**
{جملة–جملتان: "We conducted a systematic literature review following PRISMA 2020, searching …, which yielded N records, of which M were included and coded using a problem/solution taxonomy."}

**Results.**
{أهم نتيجتين أو ثلاث بالأرقام: "Findings show that hallucination (T1) accounts for X% of coded problems, while …"}

**Implications.**
{جملة للباحثين + جملة للممارسين: "For researchers, …; for practitioners, …"}
